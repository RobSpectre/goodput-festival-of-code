# Surface brief: the Hand-Inked Lap Chart treatment (docs/lap-chart/index.html)

## Scope and mode

An alternate treatment of the Goodput Festival of Code landing page, selectable from the Treatment menu in every header. Same event and facts as the Official Programme page; its own world, composition and interaction. Mode: **Persuade**.

## Audience, job, action

- Audience: AI engineers and builders first; technical leaders and buyers second.
- Job: decide whether this is a real technical event worth two days in San Francisco.
- Action: **Request an invitation**. The URL is one setting (`docs/assets/js/config.js`). While it is empty, every invitation control leads to the closing section, which says entries open shortly and makes "Save the date" its working action.
- Content: the user's programme facts only. Charts may show the meeting's real counts (2 days, 4 drivers, 3 customers, 1 record attempt, 1 machine, the 30 days of April 2027), the visitor's own forecast, and the record as an empty form. Any picture of an idea carries "illustrative, not to scale". No record figure is invented.
- Constraints: static files in `docs/lap-chart/`; shared `config.js` and `treatments.js`; no logos or photographs; original portraits, not likenesses.

## Direction contract

**THESIS.** The festival is argued the way W. E. B. Du Bois's 1900 data portraits argued: every question gets a hand-inked chart whose form is invented for it, and the honest gaps are drawn as gaps. It refuses the conference template and equally the dashboard: no axes-and-cards analytics, no numbers that were not counted.

**OWN-WORLD.** Aged cream board with four inks laid flat and saturated in fields that own real area: crimson, gold, emerald, ink black. Lines waver slightly, as if ruled by hand; the waver is drawn into the geometry by the generator, never filtered, so edges stay crisp. Shapes carry an ink keyline, colour sits inside. The forecast sheet alone is drawn true, because its lengths are measured. Type: fat hand-cut capitals for titles, a drafting hand in tracked capitals for every caption, an ink-soft serif for prose. Components: plates titled by a question, legend swatches, ruled leaders, a ruled calendar, an empty lap-chart form, a coil, wrapped bars.

**STORY.** In one screen: a two-day festival of AI inference, 27 and 28 April 2027, Palace of Fine Arts, whose main event is a record attempt nobody can chart yet. The visitor comes to believe the claims are counted, not puffed, and acts by requesting an invitation from the header, the first plate and the closing field.

**FIRST VIEWPORT.** The title hand-lettered across the top of the sheet. Under it, one plate in three columns. Middle: a single line, folded like a ribbon with mitred corners, runs as four straight bars drawn to one scale along its middle (4 drivers, 3 customers, 2 days, 1 machine) and then, in crimson, coils into a giant spiral with a question mark at its centre: tokens per second, the one quantity not yet known, drawn to no scale. Left: the date, the place, one sentence of offer, and the action. Right: the timekeeper's lap chart as a ruled form, the four racing numbers entered and every lap blank, over a note on how to read the sheet. The line inks itself in one stroke on load. On phones the order is title, date, place, action, then the line, so the question mark at the spiral's centre is on the first screen; the offer and the lap chart follow.

**FORM.** The Hand-Inked Lap Chart. seed: none (pinned). The user: "Let's do the other two treatments as well." (the Hand-Inked Lap Chart card from the decision page, catalog source `dubois-data-portraits`). Signature interaction: the visitor pencils in a forecast; it is inked as a gold coil, then unrolled into wrapped bars of exactly the same length, one sheet-width per thousand tokens per second, over the record's empty form. Motion grammar: ink drawn in one continuous stroke; ink moving from coil to bar.

**FINISH.** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Memorable moment

The first plate: bars you can count, then a crimson spiral with a question mark at its centre.
