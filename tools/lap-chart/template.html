How to read the sheet

The bars were counted. They are drawn to one scale, measured along the middle of the line.

The spiral was not. Nobody knows yet how long it is. That is what the two days are for.

What is goodput?

The part of throughput that arrives whole, and in time to be useful. In this paddock: the tokens somebody was actually waiting for. The rest is wheelspin.

For two days in April the Palace of Fine Arts becomes a paddock. The machines are NVIDIA clusters. The drivers are the people who build on them. The stopwatch reads in tokens per second.

How fast is fast?

Nobody knows yet. That is the point of an attempt.

On the day, Nebius Token Factory will go for the world record for inference speed on NVIDIA Vera Rubin, in front of the grandstand, with the timekeepers watching. The figure either stands or it does not. Until it does, the record’s line stays off this sheet.

Colophon

Drawn in four inks by a script that imitates a ruling pen; no hand touched it. The four bars on the first chart were counted, the forecast is whatever you enter, and the record is left blank. The forecast sheet is ruled to the size of the paper, not to a prediction.

The first chart follows the form of “City and Rural Population. 1890”, coloured bars folding into a spiral, a plate from The Georgia Negro: A Social Study, prepared by W. E. B. Du Bois and his students at Atlanta University for the American Negro Exhibit at the 1900 Paris Exposition. The method is borrowed with admiration; the subject is our own.
