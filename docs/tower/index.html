A race meeting for inference, scored in tokens per second.

For two days in April the Palace of Fine Arts is a paddock. The machines are NVIDIA clusters, the drivers are the people who build on them, and every watch in the tower reads in tokens per second.

Come for the record attempt. Stay for the talk in the pits, where the people who serve the world’s models compare notes on going faster without dropping anything on the way.

Goodput, n. The part of throughput that arrives whole, and in time to be useful. On this sheet: the tokens somebody was actually waiting for. The rest is wheelspin.

Timekeepers’ glossary

Standing start
Time to first token. The watch runs from the flag, not from when the engine is warm.
Flying lap
Sustained throughput, taken once everything is up to temperature.
Scrutineering
Evals. Nothing takes the line until it has been looked over.
Slipstream
Streaming. Tokens tucked in nose to tail.
Pit stop
A checkpoint. The quick ones win races.

Benchmarking is dangerous
